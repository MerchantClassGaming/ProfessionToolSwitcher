# Profession Tool Switcher

Puts on the right profession tool and accessories for the recipe you're crafting, so you don't have to dig through your bags.

## What it does

- **The right tool for every recipe.** Save a tool for each crafting stat (Resourcefulness, Multicraft, Ingenuity, Crafting Speed), pick which stat a recipe should use, and the addon switches to that tool when you select the recipe.
- **Accessories by expansion.** Save your accessories once per expansion and they go on automatically when you craft.
- **Works on crafting orders.** See and switch your tools right from the crafting orders screen.
- **Always know where you stand.** A quick status shows whether your gear matches, and the **Switch Gear** button fixes it with one click.

## Getting started

1. Open any profession window.
2. Click the **PTS** button at the top of the window.
3. Put your tools and accessories in the slots.
4. Start crafting.
